D is C taken further: what changed is listed under it

A · Quiet luxury

Take everything away until only the numbers and the sentence are left. One type family at two weights (300 and 400, never bold). Hierarchy comes from scale only: 44px, 38px, 16px, 13px. No card borders, no shadows, no badges, no icons; the side rail is a single tonal surface. Navy appears once. Lineage: Ramp's single-weight rule, Mercury's calibrated restraint.

B · Ink & signal

An inverted colour rhythm. The navy is no longer an accent card, it is the substrate: a full-bleed ink band carries the greeting, three giant figures and the account switcher. Below it, white content ruled by lines, not boxes. One bright signal (pale cyan) points at the thing that matters; coral marks trouble. Lineage: Wise's dark/light inversion, Linear's "one flashlight" accent.

C · The picture

The data landscape is the hero. The partner's fourteen-day health trend fills the top of the page in the partner's own hue with the score sitting inside it. Accounts are a strip of soft tiles with their own miniature lines; everything else is a rounded, tonal surface with an inset hairline, no shadows. Friendlier voice. Lineage: Family's warm restraint, where a picture carries the emotion and the chrome stays quiet.

D · The picture, authored

C with the SaaS removed. The chart is the page, not a card. Gone: pill nav, avatar bubbles, tile strip, rounded cards, buttons, badges, status dots, the greeting. In their place: an extrabold statement about this partner over the landscape, a 168px score in the partner's hue, figures set light, enrollment drawn as fifteen bars, an index instead of a list, numbered items with real sentences, and one action written as a sentence with an arrow.
